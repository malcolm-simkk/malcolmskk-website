A bit of background

I'm a Network Computing student at Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (UNIMAS). I'm also an undergraduate researcher, and the topics I keep coming back to are wireless networks, traditional cryptography and post-quantum cryptography.

This year I joined my first hackathons. In my very first one, the Healthcare AI Hackathon, our team made it to the final and finished 4th. Since then I've done Dormathon and NASA Space Apps, where we're now in the top 5.

I also teach. I tutor maths from Form 1 up to A Level, plus competition prep for things like Kangaroo Math and IMONST, and I coach chess for everyone from beginners to competitive players.
