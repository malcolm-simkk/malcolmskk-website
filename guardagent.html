GuardAgent

Spots a fall on camera, then calls an ambulance, unlocks the door and texts the family.

★ 4th place in the grand final. My first hackathon, and we made it all the way to the final round.

About the project

We built an AI system for elderly care that uses a camera to detect falls in real time. It assesses the fall using the person’s medical history, then calls an ambulance with a spoken briefing, unlocks the door for paramedics and texts the family.

Healthcare AI Hackathon · Kuching, Sarawak · August 2026
