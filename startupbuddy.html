StartupBuddy

Helps first-time founders in Malaysia go from a rough business idea to a proper plan and their first meetings.

About the project

We built an AI tool to help aspiring Malaysian founders test a business idea and book their first meetings. It checks the idea against Malaysian law, finds nearby competitors, builds a Business Model Canvas for a panel of AI reviewers, and drafts emails and calendar invitations once the founder approves.
