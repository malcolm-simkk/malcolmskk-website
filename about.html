A bit of background

I’m an undergraduate student studying Network Computing at Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (UNIMAS). As an undergraduate researcher, I’m exploring wireless network technology, traditional cryptography, and post-quantum cryptography.

Alongside these interests, I provide mathematics tutoring for school and pre-university students, including Competitive Mathematics preparation for competitions such as Kangaroo Math and IMONST. I also offer chess coaching for every level.

You can find details of my lessons here, or get in touch if you’d like to talk about research.
